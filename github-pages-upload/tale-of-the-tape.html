Methodology (v0)

Corpus. OTB classical games from Lichess broadcasts of major events (Candidates, world championship, Norway Chess, Tata Steel Masters, Grand Chess Tour, Olympiad, Grand Swiss and similar), Jan 2024 onward. Clock data confirms classical play; rapid/blitz/armageddon segments are excluded.

Prep depth = first move with a think over 5 minutes (median). It's a proxy: fast play can mean preparation or familiar ground by habit. Opening pace = % of starting clock left after move 15, normalized per game. Time trouble = share of moves 20–40 played with under 5 minutes. Variety = Shannon entropy over the first 10 plies, normalized by the maximum possible for that sample size (raw entropy comparisons are biased when players have different game counts; that's also why it has no CI — naive bootstrap entropy is biased low).

"Clear edge" vs "about even": a difference is called clear only when the bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (1,000 resamples) don't overlap. Overlapping intervals mean the data can't separate the players — saying so is the honest answer, not a cop-out.
