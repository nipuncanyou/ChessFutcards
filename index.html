THE SIX STATS

HOW THE CARD READS YOU.

Your overall comes from where your rating sits against everyone else on the site. The six stats then move around it based on how you actually play your recent rated games.

SPD

Speed

Your bullet rating against your slower ones, and how often you lose on the clock.

Top stat: Speed Demon ♞︎
ATK

Attack

How many of your wins end in checkmate, and how many of your games end decisively.

Top stat: Tactician ♛︎
DEF

Defense

How you score against players rated above you.

Top stat: The Wall ♜︎
END

Endgame

How you do once a game goes past move 35, compared with your usual results.

Top stat: Grinder ♚︎
OPN

Openings

How many different openings you play across your recent games.

Top stat: Theory Nerd ♝︎
CON

Consistency

How you play in the game right after a loss. Ice in the veins, or tilt.

Top stat: Untiltable ♟︎
