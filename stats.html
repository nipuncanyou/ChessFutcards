HOW THE RATINGS WORK

No black box. Every number on your card comes from your public games and ratings — here's exactly how, and if you've generated a card, exactly why yours came out the way it did.

OVR Overall

Your overall is calibrated to percentiles — how you rank against everyone on your platform, not just your rating number. A 75 means roughly "clearly better than most people on the site", not "75% of a grandmaster".

At the very top the percentile scale saturates (everyone elite is 99.9th percentile), so above 93 the scale switches to absolute rating. That's why a 3400-rated super-GM gets a 99 while a strong titled streamer lands 93–94 — no more everyone-is-96 ties.

Formula: best of blitz/rapid percentile → 40–93; ratings above ~2500 stretch 93→99 by absolute rating. Lichess percentiles come live from the Lichess API; Chess.com uses an approximate distribution.

Tiers & rarity

Card look follows FUT conventions: bronze under 65, silver 65–74, gold 75 and up. The shinier rare finish goes to the top of each tier and to every titled player. Special cards override the base tiers.

SPD Speed

How dangerous you are when the clock matters. Driven by your bullet strength relative to your slower ratings, plus how rarely you lose on time.

Bullet percentile vs your overall base, minus a penalty if a big share of your losses are flags (baseline ~18% of losses).

ATK Attack

Killer instinct. Do your wins end in checkmate, or do opponents just resign or flag eventually? Plus how often your games are decisive at all.

Share of wins by checkmate (baseline ~28%) + decisive-game rate (baseline ~62%).

DEF Defense

Upset resistance: how you score against opponents rated above you. Great defenders drag stronger players into the mud and take their points.

Score in games vs opponents 25+ points higher (expected baseline ~40%). Needs at least 8 such games, otherwise DEF stays at your overall.

END Endgame

Do long games favor you? Compares your score in games past move 35 against your overall score — grinders go up, players who thrive on quick chaos go down.

Score in 70+ ply games minus overall score. Needs 8+ long games.

OPN Openings

How hard you are to prepare against: the variety of openings you actually play. One-trick London players, this is where it shows.

Distinct openings (ECO codes) divided by games played (baseline ~55%).

CON Consistency

Tilt resistance. After you lose a game, do you bounce back or spiral? Measured from the game played immediately after each loss.

Score in post-loss games minus overall score. Needs 6+ post-loss games.

The honest caveats

Sub-stats flex a few points around your overall (±7) — they show style, not independent skills. Accounts with fewer than 10 recent rated games get a ratings-only "mystery card". Chess.com percentiles are approximate. This is a fan project for fun, not a FIDE instrument.
